Spaghetti recipe

Spaghetti is a national dish originated from Italy. has a mild, earthy, and slightly nutty wheat flavor with a chewy, firm texture

It is easy to do. Brown your meat in a bit of oil, and then stir in onions until they soften. Then add lots of flavor with garlic, tomato paste, oregano, and red pepper flakes. To finish the sauce, add a bit of water (or broth) and crushed tomatoes. You only need to simmer the sauce for about 25 minutes, so about 15 minutes before it is ready, you can cook your spaghetti.
