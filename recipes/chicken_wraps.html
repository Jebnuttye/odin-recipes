Chicken wraps

A chicken wrap is a popular, portable handheld meal made by rolling seasoned chicken and fresh fillings inside a soft flatbread or tortilla.

Ingredients

  • Boneless chicken tits
  • Spices of your liking (for example: paprika, garlic black pepper, salt)
  • Tortillas
  • Vegetables of your choice
  • Cheese of your choice
  • Garlic sauce

Steps

  1. Cut your chicken into small cubes. Add spices. Cook/Fry it up till done
  2. Add charlic sauce on top of your tortilla. On top of that sauce add mix of cooked meat and vegetables. Top it off with cheese and close the tortilla.
